Going the Extra Mile

The numbers come from how the three work. They take intervention calls and counsel candidates who stall — tracker notes show days of repeated calls and messages to bring paused candidates back — and personalise feedback and sessions to each candidate's gaps. They adjust their own schedules around working professionals, running mocks in evenings and on weekends, and hand-hold past graduation to the first class (see Kshitiz, Pooja and Akshay above). Ownership beyond their own KPIs: in crunch weeks they step forward to role-play and review for other MathFit Mentors' candidates so the Academy lands a solid final number of MathFit Certified Coaches — 123 Final Evaluation reviews on other Mentors' mentees this period (Prachi 63, Anuva 42, Neethu 18), none counting toward their own totals — together delivering 400 MathFit Certified Coaches in JAS 2026.
