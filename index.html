Program Impact

In Q3 2026, Trial Training absorbed a record intake of 716 candidates (287 in September alone) while the training flow moved from RISE 3.0 to the new Training Tracker — and graduated 400 teachers, the programme's best quarter yet.

Reason for Nomination
  • Delivery at scale — the three carried 44% of the programme's candidates and delivered 47% of its graduates: 186 of 400.
  • Quality held under load — throughput 59% vs 52% and drop rate 17% vs 23% against the other five core mentors, with rubric scoring as strict as any mentor's (1–2% fail rates).
  • Consistency through change — 532 of the 1,170 mock-session reviews this quarter, feedback within two days, and the highest mentee ratings surveyed on being essential to clearing the Final Trial.

Going the Extra Mile

The numbers come from how the three work. They take intervention calls and counsel candidates who stall — tracker notes show days of repeated calls and messages to bring paused candidates back — and personalise feedback and sessions to each candidate's gaps. They adjust their own schedules around working professionals, running mocks in evenings and on weekends ("she said don't worry, I will manage time for you on weekends to accommodate your office timings"; "she scheduled my demo as per my time preference and took her personal time to fill in my doubts"). And they hand-hold past graduation to the first class ("even now, whenever I have a doubt, she always responds"; "after my final trial she took 15 minutes just to ask what the problem was") — together making 400 teachers Business Ready in JAS 2026.
