Program Impact

Reason for Nomination
  • Delivery at scale — the three carried 44% of the programme's candidates and delivered 47% of its graduates: 186 of 400.
  • Quality held under load — throughput 59% vs 52% and drop rate 17% vs 23% against the other five core mentors, with rubric scoring as strict as any mentor's (1–2% fail rates).
  • Consistency through change — 532 of the 1,170 mock-session reviews this quarter, feedback within two days, and the highest mentee ratings surveyed on being essential to clearing the Final Trial.

Going the Extra Mile

The numbers come from how the three work. They take intervention calls and counsel candidates who stall — tracker notes show days of repeated calls and messages to bring paused candidates back — and personalise feedback and sessions to each candidate's gaps. They adjust their own schedules around working professionals, running mocks in evenings and on weekends, and hand-hold past graduation to the first class (see Kshitiz, Pooja and Akshay above). Ownership beyond their own KPIs: in crunch weeks they step forward to role-play and review for other mentors' candidates so the programme lands a solid final number of certified coaches — 123 Final Evaluation reviews on other mentors' mentees this period (Prachi 63, Anuva 42, Neethu 18), none counting toward their own totals — together making 400 teachers Business Ready in JAS 2026.
